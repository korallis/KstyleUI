# KstyleUI release notes

The player-facing notes for every release are in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md). The packager reads that file, so the GitHub release carries the whole file. The owner tags a release on `main` after the in-game test of `staging` passes, as [`TESTING.md`](TESTING.md) describes.

## 0.3.4.1: party frames show each buff once (2026-11-24)

Thanks to Baine for spotting this. Party and raid frames could show the same buff twice on a member, like Find Minerals on a paladin or a zone buff on a priest, because the External and Big defensives indicators each picked up the member's top buff. Those two indicators only light up for real defensives, and Forever doesn't report defensives yet, so they stay empty for now and keep their place on the frame. Pick either one under Options > Party and Raid > Indicators and a note tells you it isn't available on this client. An indicator set to the buffs you choose by spell ID still shows that buff, once. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.3.4: a crest for every class (2026-11-23)

Every class now has its own crest, one place to look. The crest tracks what your class watches: your buffs, what you put on the target, your procs and your cooldowns. A piece shows once you know its spell, so a low-level character sees only what it has. Every piece is on by default with its own switch under Action Bars > Slab and cooldowns, and under Frames the same pieces stand as medals in a row above the stance bar.

A warrior sees Battle Shout, the strike you can use right now in each stance, Enrage burning round the inner rim, your main strike and its procs on the hips, your interrupt and Charge on the feet (Taunt in Defensive Stance) and a threat gem that glows while you hold the target. A paladin gets the Seal above the crest, Judgement ready and on the target, Vengeance or Holy Shield charges on the jewels, what your spec needs on the hips and Hammer of Justice on the feet. A rogue gets Slice and Dice and Rupture on the shoulders, a vial per weapon that drains with its poison, Kick and your spec's piece on the feet, and in Stealth the opener you can use and Vanish's cooldown.

Hunters get the Aspect, the pet's portrait with its health, happiness and Mend Pet, Hunter's Mark and your newest Sting on the feet, a ring that drains over each Auto Shot and turns amber when ammo runs low, and a gust while Rapid Fire or Quick Shots lasts. The Sting and Rapid Fire tracking is there thanks to the hunters in Discord. Mages get their armor and mana gem on the shoulders, Counterspell and Fire Vulnerability or Winter's Chill on the hips, Frost Nova and your newest spell on the target at the feet, a ring for Ice Barrier or Mana Shield and a blaze for Clearcasting. Warlocks get their DoTs as four spires that burn down on the crown, the demon, Healthstone, weapon stone, Conflagrate, Curse, Spell Lock and a blaze for Shadow Trance. Soul Shards are items in your bags on Forever, not a power, so the crest counts them across every bag, soul bag included, and shows them as wisps.

Priests get Inner Fire as four flames that burn down as hits spend its charges, Penance (Mind Blast for shadow), Shadowfiend and Shadowform, Shadow Word: Pain on the top gem and your Power Word: Shield, Renew and Prayer of Mending on the target. Shadow priests also get Devouring Plague, Shadow Word: Death and Silence. Druids get a full crest in every form: Moonfire or Rejuvenation and Insect Swarm or Regrowth as a caster, Rake and Rip in Cat Form, and Demoralizing Roar, Faerie Fire, Growl, Feral Charge, Maul and a gold tank glow in Bear Form, with Clearcasting leaves round the rim. Shamans get a rune stone per weapon that glows in its imbue's element, a sweep for the shock cooldown, Flame Shock, Stormstrike or Riptide on the target and Reincarnation's cooldown, greyed without an Ankh.

Every crest also burns your major cooldowns round the rim, with up to three running ones below the emblem, and mana users see the five-second rule as a blue ring round the level badge. Charge and stack counts sit inside their medal.

Thanks to Baine for the report behind three world map fixes. Closing the map clears its search, so an old search list or Nearest picker no longer comes back when you reopen it after viewing another zone. The Search the map box moved to the map's top-right corner, clear of Blizzard's map pin button and other map addons, and zone level ranges on continent maps keep their size whether the map is windowed or maximized. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

Thanks to Baine for spotting these. The rest comes from the native-size pass. A tracked buff bar's stack count sits inside its icon, and in Medallions on wide screens the consumables bar sits beside your player frame. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.3.3: fit and finish (2026-11-23)

This one is all about the finish. Party frames were hard to read on a big screen, the Medallions unit frames didn't properly fit the crest, and a native-size pass over the whole UI turned up a lot of small things sitting a few pixels off. All of that is fixed here. The crest trackers for every class aren't in this release; they're coming in v0.3.4.

Party frames on ultrawide screens (21:9 and wider) are a quarter larger, unless you set their size yourself. Health percentages no longer cut 100% down to 10..., party names sit on a darker backing with a soft halo, and someone out of range fades less, so their name stays readable. The missing part of a health bar stays dark, without the pale band for health just lost, so 45% reads as 45%, and offline and dead members show empty bars instead of looking healthy. The percentages and the dark track reach raid frames too. Each part has its own switch under Party and Raid > Readability: Larger party frames, Readable names and numbers and Dark missing health, all on by default.

On Medallions, the player and target slabs now dock into the crest ring instead of stopping short of it, and the shaman's Ghost Wolf and Call medals sit on them like rivets. Health and power sit evenly inside the slab, with power in its own colour and no stray line past its end, and the focus frame gets the same bars. Leader, role, PvP, combat and resting icons sit in steel sockets along the slab's top edge, an empty slot on the player shows a small rivet so the row stays even, and the raid marker sits on the portrait's rim clear of the target's debuff timers. Cast bars on these frames start clear of the portrait ring. Unit Frames > Fitted slabs turns it all back to the earlier slabs. Incoming heals now draw in the health bar's own colour instead of a green chunk, on every unit frame, under Unit Frames > Heal prediction in the bar's colour. Aura stack counts sit inside their icon's corner, and boss and focus cast bars show their track.

The polish pass covers action bar keybinds and pet autocast dots, the leave-vehicle button, the crest travel menu, the minimap coordinates plaque, nameplate threat marks, the chat input box, bag cooldowns and item levels, comparison tooltips, the data bars, the swing timer and meters, the quest tracker and quest card, the spellbook, merchant names, Options and setup. Two of those come with a switch: bag cooldowns show as the swipe alone under Bags > Swipe-only cooldowns, and the honour bar matches the experience and reputation bars in Medallions under Data Bars > Honour bar > Match the other bars.

RestedXP 4.11.39 renamed a few things the skin looked for, so KstyleUI skipped its RestedXP skin entirely on that version. It works again: the theme, the guide card, the arrow and the docked boxes all apply, and the boxes stay docked when RestedXP's scale changes. Those target and item boxes are small icon chips now instead of wide empty panels, the guide configurator's buttons form one even row, and the arrow's text makes way while the game shows the name of the zone you enter, under Window Skins > RestedXP > Arrow text makes way for zone names. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.3.2: gamepad controls (2026-11-23)

This is the gamepad release. While you play with a controller, your action bar keybinds show its buttons instead of key names: Xbox, PlayStation or Switch, as the game draws them, with Shift and Ctrl as the left and right triggers. A pad key the game has no button picture for shows its own name, and with the keyboard in your hands nothing changes. Action Bars > Bars > Controller button icons turns it off; it is on by default.

Setup and the General page get a Controls choice under HUD style. Gamepad puts eight buttons on bars 1 and 2, each split into a left and a right group of four to match the controller, and on Frames it also drops the tray so both bars get bigger buttons. Keyboard puts the bars back the way KstyleUI ships them. Neither one moves anything. General > Setup > Keyboard or gamepad bars hides the choice and leaves your bars alone.

The shaman totem bar's choice fan finally gets the forged look from the Spirit Poles design. An open fan sits on a dark band rimmed in the element's colour, with a steel lip on the Medallions crest, and every choice wears its element's edge and tab. Under Frames each fan column now starts above its socket's spire, so the timer on the totem you have down stays in sight while you pick the next one.

The consumables bar starts at button size 26 with no gap between buttons. Sizes and spacings you set yourself stay, and Spacing -1 still gives you the HUD style's gap. If you moved the bar off the player frame and never changed its size, it grows around where you left it, so check it is not sitting on another bar in Layout Mode.

In v0.3.1 the setup window's pages, starting with Welcome, could stay blank in game after `/kstyle setup`, and Options pages could do the same when you switched page. Both show again. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.3.1: item levels and glass on Blizzard bars (2026-11-23)

Your gear shows its item level now. The character window puts each piece's level in the top left of its slot, in its quality colour, and the inspect window does the same for the player you inspect once their gear loads. The shirt, tabard and ammo slots get no number. The numbers use the same corner, face and colours as the bags, which already had them under Bags > Item levels. Window Skins > Character sheet > Item levels turns them off on both windows; it is on by default.

The lit glass finish from v0.3.0 now reaches the Blizzard bars KstyleUI restyles: the trainer's skill bar, the Legacy window's reward and challenge bars, RestedXP's guide import bar and the quest tracker's timer and progress bars. They follow the same switch as KstyleUI's own bars, General > Appearance > Lit glass bars, and with it off those windows look exactly as they did in v0.3.0.

Two v0.3.0 bugs are fixed. World map routes name every step: a step that ends between named places takes the nearest town or area, and the last step names where you are going, so the nearest innkeeper route ends with Road to Innkeeper Grosk instead of Road to and nothing after it. Routes also stop offering a hearthstone with no place after it when the game gives no bind location. And a debuff you can dispel on a party or raid member tints their frame at your Highlight fill opacity again, under Party and Raid > Debuffs, instead of covering it in a solid colour that hid the name and health. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.3.0: polish and the explorer map (2026-11-23)

This is the polish release and the explorer release shipped together. Everything new is on by default except Reveal unexplored areas, and every piece has its own switch, so turning one off gives you that part of the old look back exactly.

A clean screen at rest is the biggest change you will notice. Out of combat with no target, not casting, with your power full and your health no longer climbing, your action bars, player frame, data strip and KstyleUI's own cooldown rows fade to 35% after three seconds. They come straight back when you target something, cast, enter combat, take damage, open Layout Mode, drag a spell or point at them, and pointing brings back just that panel. A bar you set to fade until hovered keeps its own fade. Elements > Clean screen at rest turns it off, sets Rest opacity and picks which panels fade.

Every KstyleUI bar now shares one lit glass finish: a soft light along the top, a thin bright edge and depth toward the bottom, turned on its side for bars that stand upright. It covers health, power and cast bars on your unit frames, party and raid frames and nameplates, plus the experience and reputation bars, swing timer, tracked buff bars and movement bars. Damage and threat meter bars only get the light, so class colours stay full strength. Medallions names, levels and cast names share one number face with the rest of the HUD, and a soft glow behind nameplate names and a drop shadow under timers and counts keep text readable over bright ground and spell effects. All three live under General > Appearance: Lit glass bars, One typeface voice and Soft glow behind world text.

Medallions unit frames and cast bars end in forged steel caps lit like the crest's ring, and cast bars end in a blade tip; Unit Frames > Forged slab ends turns them back to the rounded ends. Out of combat with no target the crest settles to a slow breath and wakes the moment you enter combat, pick a target or get a proc; General > Medallions > Calm crest at rest keeps it fully animated when off.

Target and focus cast bars, on their frames and on nameplates, show who the spell is aimed at in their class colour, or YOU when it is you. Casts you cannot interrupt carry a shield. While your interrupt recharges the bar dims, counts down the seconds and puts a gold mark where your interrupt comes back, and an interrupted cast briefly names who interrupted it. The switch is Cast targets and interrupts, once under Unit Frames and once under Nameplates > Cast bar.

The world map becomes a travel companion. It remembers the innkeepers, trainers, banks, auction houses, repair and reagent vendors you visit, pins them, and with the search box empty offers Nearest innkeeper, Nearest bank and the rest; nothing is shipped or guessed, so a service shows up once one of your characters has talked to it. Clicking a quest on the quest card routes to it, or tells you when the game gives no position. Routes now use zeppelins, the Deeprun Tram and the boats between Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms, so a place on the other continent gets a full route, and they take your hearthstone, Astral Recall and the teleports and portals you know when that is faster, counting any cooldown still running. Boat and zeppelin steps are named after their town, like Boat to Booty Bay. Nearest services, Click a quest to route and Travel stops are under Window Skins > World map, on by default. Reveal unexplored areas, in the same place, draws the parts of the map you have not explored yet, dimmed so you can still tell them apart; it starts off because it changes what the map shows.

Options search takes you there: press Enter or click a result's name and the window opens that page at the setting, picks the right frame or bar and lights the row for a moment. It knows everyday words too, so "hp" finds health settings, "color" finds colour and "kick" finds interrupts. The Elements page shows a small map of your screen with every element at its own opacity, and the Profiles page shows your text and colours. Menus, settings pages and Layout Mode open, close and fade with the same timing as the settings window, and Layout Mode's snap guides are thicker, fade in as an element snaps and hold the element still while you drag inside the snap. The switches are under General > Window conveniences, and Clear snap guides is under Elements > Mover controls.

The first run gets better as well. Setup shows one big picture of each layout in your HUD style, and on ultrawide screens pictures of Centred and Spread to pick from. One-click Looks, in setup and on the General page under HUD style, offer Forged (the usual look), Clean and Readable. General > Setup > Big pictures in setup and One-click Looks turn them off.

KstyleUI now tells you once after login if part of it is turned off, missing, out of date or failed to load, and offers the fix: turn it back on and reload, reload, or a download link to copy. About > Status shows how every part is doing and gives you a report to copy into Discord, and About > Status > Load check turns the notice off. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.2.10: the frame-rate release (2026-11-21)

KstyleUI now draws less every frame. The see-through middles of its rings, outlines and frame borders are no longer drawn, which takes about a fifth of the drawn area off Frames, a quarter off Medallions and a tenth off Orbs, and nothing looks different. Two new switches under General > Medallions trade effects for frame rate: Low-health vignette takes the red screen edge at low health out of the drawing while your portrait's danger ring stays, and Ambient hub effects stills the hub's turning runes, breathing glow, sheen and sparks while combat, proc and cast flashes still play. `/kstyle perf`, contributed by Baine, records your frame rate and the Lua time KstyleUI spends for 20 seconds or the length you give, then writes the costliest work first to the devlog. ### Detailed changes

- Your player and target frames are dark steel slabs with clean bars and a round medallion on the outer end. Your medallion shows a painted portrait of your race, class and sex, and so does any other player you target. Creatures, pets and bosses show the game's own portrait. Your focus, pet and boss frames get medallions too, and your target's target and your focus's target are small slabs.
- At the centre of the slab is a crest painted for your race and class, 52 in all, in a ring of runes in your class colour. At rest it breathes, its runes turn and your class's particles drift round it. When you enter combat its wings spread. It pulses when an ability beside it lights up, and it bursts when you level up. The crest always sits under your action buttons, so it never hides a spell, a cooldown or a keybind.
- Every effect answers something that happened. A slot flashes steel when its cooldown comes back. Your cast bar flashes when a cast lands, and flashes red when a cast is stopped, interrupted or fails. A red edge grows round the screen when your health runs low. The crest's ring lights up while your rage, energy or focus is full; a paladin's follows holy power and a warlock's soul shards, and mana never lights it. A burst blooms behind your portrait when you level up. Three seconds after combat, your pet, focus, focus target and target-of-target frames dim to 60%, and they come back when the next fight starts.
- **Medallions motion** in Options > General > Medallions sets how much moves: Full (the default) adds the game's own 3D spell effects for procs and level-ups, Calm keeps the slow motion and short flashes, and Off stops the motion, though the low-health edge and the crest's ring still show and the frames still dim after combat. Options > General > Appearance > Motion still wins: Reduced caps Medallions motion at Calm, and Off turns it off.
- The rest of the screen follows. Cards, windows, tooltips and menus turn to steel, with your class colour on their headers. The minimap turns round in a steel ring. Party members get painted badges, raid frames become class-coloured tiles, and friendly players' nameplates carry a small badge. Your cast bars hang under their slabs, and your experience and reputation show as thin strips under the action slab.

## 0.0.5: the first public release

KstyleUI restyles the whole World of Warcraft: Forever interface in one Diablo IV-style forged look: dark cards and windows with forged iron brackets (the card material is a setting), a type set you choose in Options (Folio by default, Lumen as an alternative) and forged ornaments at Off, Subtle or Full. The default layout lines the interface up in rails: chat and your party on the left, your hands at the centre, and the map, buffs, readout and story card on the right. It needs Forever (TOC `16001`). The repainted icons are an optional second download, `KstyleUI-Icons-<version>.zip`; see [`icons.md`](icons.md).

### Safety and performance

- Every module is off until you switch it on, and a module that is off creates no frames and registers no events.
- KstyleUI never compares or does arithmetic on the game's secret values: health, power, casts and aura data go straight to the game's widgets.
- Protected changes made in combat wait for combat to end. Blizzard's frames are hidden by moving them to a hidden parent outside combat and Edit Mode, never by scripting them.
- Action bars, group frames, bags and auras hand back to Blizzard's in the gamepad interface.
- Combat paths are measured offline against a time budget and must allocate no memory.

### Known limitations

- Several engine behaviours are confirmed only in game. Where KstyleUI cannot confirm one, it takes a safer path and writes which path it took to the devlog.
- The nameplate distance settings show only on a client that has those settings.
- A gamepad layout, extra themes, boss timelines and translations beyond the five shipped languages are not part of this release. The German, French, Spanish and Simplified Chinese tables are machine-translated and unreviewed by native speakers.

### Detailed changes

- Every part of the interface as a module you switch on when you want it: unit frames, group frames, action bars, cooldowns, buffs and debuffs, nameplates, combat text, minimap, chat, bags and bank, tooltips, data bars, meters, window skins and a quality-of-life module.
- A settings window (`/kstyle`) with a page per module, search across every setting and a live preview, per-character profiles you can export and import as a string, and a first-run setup that picks a DPS, Tank or Healer layout and your modules.
- Forged ornaments at Off, Subtle or Full (Full by default), on unit frames and cast bars, the action bar tray, nameplates, the minimap, the experience, reputation and honour bars, windows, tooltips and the RestedXP windows. They are dropped where they do not fit, never squeezed.
- Layouts follow one reference picture: chat and your party on the left, your hands at the centre, and the map, buffs, bars, readout and story card on the right. DPS, Tank and Healer layouts do not overlap at 16:9, 16:10 or 21:9, and everything sits on whole screen pixels at every size.
