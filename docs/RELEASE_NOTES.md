# KstyleUI release notes

The player-facing notes for every release are in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md). The packager reads that file, so the GitHub release carries the whole file. The owner tags a release on `main` after the in-game test of `staging` passes, as [`TESTING.md`](TESTING.md) describes.

## 0.2.8: the map and bars release (2026-11-20)

The world map zooms smoothly with the mouse wheel, remembers each map's zoom and pan, and wheels out to the parent map or in to the zone under the cursor (Smooth zoom, Remember zoom and Wheel between maps are separate switches under Window Skins > World map, all on by default). Search finds flight masters, dungeon and raid entrances and your quests, and picking a result sets a waypoint and tracks it; a route card beside the quest list shows yards and rough travel time with a dotted straight line to the destination. Routes are straight lines in this build and the card says so; road-following routes are coming. Dungeon and zone level ranges and dungeon entrances are separate switches beside Search and routes. The first-run setup gains a "Your addons" step listing the supported addons you have installed, so you choose which ones KstyleUI skins and which parts step aside for an addon doing the same job; every skin is on by default now, and skins you switched off yourself stay off. Action Bars options open on tabs for Bar 1-6, Pet, Stance and Consumables, the picker stays pinned while you scroll, and double-clicking a bar's mover opens its settings. Each bar gains text placement (corner, edge or centre with offsets for keybind, count, macro and cooldown text), a cooldown look (colour fade on real cooldowns and swipe darkness), item quality borders for uncommon and better items on by default, a show/hide builder for combat, target, mounted, group and modifier conditions with hand-written macro conditions still available, Toggle Action Bar keys under Key Bindings > KstyleUI that save per character, and Click through and Reverse button order, both off by default. The consumables bar gains a button-count limit. Paragon reputation progress shows with an optional reward marker, and Questie's tracker can wear a KstyleUI card and header.

The consumables bar starts at half size with a backdrop; under 30 units its counts are outlined, the cooldown timer replaces the count, and keybind text is off by default, with saved size, backing and keybind choices kept. Fixes from a long gameplay test cover RestedXP's guide card fitting its steps with consistent headers and no stray gutter tick, Layout Mode labels, toolbar and thin movers that never overlap, the Medallions power value back inside its card, and no bogus frame rate just after a loading screen. Further fixes cover the durability doll moving clear of the minimap only when you never moved it, Options Tasks help and previews, percentages rounding like Blizzard, stepping aside for Leatrix Plus features and BetterFriendlist, Questie and Speedrun Guide headers, spark-free party and raid bars with tracked bars at the shared height, and login notices that show once. Upgrading turns skins on only where you never chose, and shrinks the consumables bar only where you never sized it. The optional **KstyleUI Icons** pack still applies from the GitHub releases page. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.2.7: the data bar, consumables and crest release (2026-11-20)

A data bar runs across the top in Frames and Orbs with your specialization, Hearthstone and a toggleable micro menu on the left, the clock in the centre, and guild, friends, durability, bag space, frame rate, latency, gold and tracked currencies on the right; at its default 14 units Frames and Orbs keep their v0.2.6 size and place, and Right rail brings back the old readout. Under Medallions a dock beneath the action slab shows the original five readouts and the micro menu, and the minimap clock steps aside while a bar shows the time. An automatic consumables bar fills itself with your best food, potions and other consumables in the shared action-bar sockets. Under Medallions your cooldowns sit centred above the crest, your buffs and your target debuffs stand in rows above their frames (Crest fan brings back the fan), you can left-click the crest to hearth and right-click it for travel spells, items and mounts, frame the minimap like the crest (Crest medallion, Crest ring or Classic), and pick an extra-bar layout of two rows on the slab or small bars beside it; every action bar can be sized and arranged in Options in every HUD style. Text options bring font, size, outline, colour and tag formats such as `[hp:abr]` and `[hp:pct]` to unit frames and nameplates, aura filtering is one shared set with spell-ID lists on nameplates, bar texture, colour and height are one shared options set, each spec can keep its own layout, any element can show only in a party, a raid or solo, and the meter follows your role, with threat beside damage for damage dealers. Meters use nameplate-style rows on 250-unit cards, nameplates share the interface's bar and fonts, and every card shares one header. Layout Mode gains Shift axis lock, stacked-frame cycling and coordinates, and any element can attach to another and match its width. Hovering a quest shows your party's progress on it.

Fixes include the alt-tab stutter, the frame-rate drop while other addons draw minimap pins, HUD parts that overlapped or left the screen at any size (saved positions are kept, and anything saved within 17 units of a screen edge is drawn just inside it), shrunken minimap icons, the Medallions damage meter's rail place and whole spell names, whole lines in the Medallions quest card, Questie and RestedXP cards drawing over other windows, dragging a spell casting it, trainer rows spilling, a blank health orb from saved Frames text settings, rested experience past one level, and percents reading one low. Running setup again with Right rail chosen keeps the readout in its old spot, the data bar's clock clears the centre gem, the Medallions dock separates its readouts from the XP caption, the Medallions quest and guide cards line up, and Right rail shows the classic readout inside its frame. When another addon does the same job KstyleUI now steps aside only for that part, and turning nameplates off gives your own game settings back. Upgrading moves Frames and Orbs readouts into the top bar and Medallions readouts into the dock, keeps the crest fan if you had moved it, docks RestedXP's item and target boxes again once, and leaves role-following meters off until you turn them on. An optional icon pack repaints ability and item icons everywhere, including buffs; the addon repaints the frames and **KstyleUI Icons** repaints the icons inside them, and the icons were made with AI image tools. The pack is not an addon and is not part of this download: it is the `KstyleUI-Icons-<version>.zip` on this project's GitHub releases page. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

Tooltips follow your mouse out of combat and sit at their usual place in combat, and that is the new default for existing players too: if you had chosen Tooltip anchor, choose it again in Options, because older versions did not keep that choice at logout. The Personal Resource Display is off by default for new setups. Enemy casts are named on target and focus plates with a shield for casts you cannot interrupt, nameplate debuffs are larger with outlined time left, there is a Dungeon nameplate preset, and Frames and Medallions show power numbers by default. Failed and interrupted casts stay readable, a finished channel is acknowledged, and low-health danger sits at the screen edges with a ring around your portrait, with options to bring back the old glow. Colour-vision choices, role and threat shapes, interrupt shields and debuff letters keep signals readable without colour alone, with Readable and Healer presets, range cues, threat alarms and resurrection, summon and AFK marks. Under Medallions your target debuffs fan above the crest, empty action sockets fade, and the Cooldown Manager and swing timer sit with the rest of the HUD. Orbs and Frames finish with their own switches, all on by default: sharp orb glass, a ledge under the split bar 2 and room for long spell names under Orbs; the spell name inside the cast bar and the tray on the centre column under Frames. Four readability switches in Appearance keep the original look one switch away, motion is one Full, Calm or Off choice for every HUD style, Options opens on common tasks with player-word search, bind-keys buttons, typed slider values and profile sharing with recovery backups, the first-run setup opens on a branded welcome page and shows each role in your chosen style, learning a new spell offers to put it on your bars, and KstyleUI skips repeated work that could not change anything on screen.

Fixes include party and raid frames the moment you join a group, six- and twelve-button third action bars that fit every HUD style, dropping a held item onto the empty slots of a grouped bag, chat tabs and buttons that line up at every size including 4K, minimap tooltips beside the map, windows that keep their buttons, prices and captions in place when reopened, long names that stay in their face and capitals at every resolution, key labels and cooldown countdowns that stay inside their buttons, aura timers that end cleanly, and healthy enemies no longer wearing a danger rim. An optional icon pack repaints ability and item icons everywhere, including buffs; the icons were made with AI image tools, and the pack is not an addon and is not part of this download: it is available from this project's GitHub page. ## 0.2.2: readable Medallions and one combat-text display

The crest hub keeps its painted steel and runes visible between effects: changing its tint no longer revives the shock ring and square flash. Medallions bars are flat, without the white sheen, and bright health fills get dark, readable numbers. CombatText now hides Blizzard's separate world damage and healing numbers by default; a saved Keep choice remains unchanged. With Keep selected, the native numbers use the combat-text font role after a full logout to the character screen and login. `/reload` alone cannot refresh that font.

The intermittent blank quest card remains under investigation. If it goes blank, `/kstyle test` records its native visibility, opacity, frame levels and geometry. Zero-visible-row layouts also record a bounded, deduplicated diagnostic automatically. Reloading or logging out saves that devlog for inspection; these diagnostics do not change the card's layout.

## 0.0.5: the first public release

KstyleUI restyles the whole World of Warcraft: Forever interface in one Diablo IV-style forged look: dark cards and windows with forged iron brackets (the card material is a setting), a type set you choose in Options (Folio by default, Lumen as an alternative) and forged ornaments at Off, Subtle or Full. The repainted icons are an optional second download, `KstyleUI-Icons-<version>.zip`; see [`icons.md`](icons.md).

### Safety and performance

- Every module is off until you switch it on, and a module that is off creates no frames and registers no events.
- KstyleUI never compares or does arithmetic on the game's secret values: health, power, casts and aura data go straight to the game's widgets.
- Protected changes made in combat wait for combat to end. Blizzard's frames are hidden by moving them to a hidden parent outside combat and Edit Mode, never by scripting them.
- Action bars, group frames, bags and auras hand back to Blizzard's in the gamepad interface.
- Combat paths are measured offline against a time budget and must allocate no memory.

### Known limitations

- Several engine behaviours are confirmed only in game. Where KstyleUI cannot confirm one, it takes a safer path and writes which path it took to the devlog.
- The nameplate distance settings show only on a client that has those settings.
- A gamepad layout, extra themes, boss timelines and translations beyond the five shipped languages are not part of this release. The German, French, Spanish and Simplified Chinese tables are machine-translated and unreviewed by native speakers.
