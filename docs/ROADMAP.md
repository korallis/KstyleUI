# KstyleUI roadmap

What's coming next, release by release. Dates are estimates; we'll keep this post up to date as each release ships. v0.3.4 is out now: every class gets its own crest, with its buffs, procs, debuffs and major cooldowns in one place, plus world map fixes. New releases land about once a day.

## v0.3.5 — the crest, tuned in game

Estimated: within a few days.

The crest pieces checked on the live client and tuned from your feedback, plus your trinkets on the rim.

Highlights:

- Anything on the crest that doesn't behave in game gets fixed, class by class.
- Your on-use trinkets burn round the rim next to your major cooldowns.
- Energy users get a tick spark on the energy bar.
- Aura timers about to run out stay easy to read over any scenery.
