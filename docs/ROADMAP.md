# KstyleUI roadmap

What's coming next, release by release. Dates are estimates; we'll keep this post up to date as each release ships. v0.3.3 is out now: readable party frames, Medallions frames that fit the crest, a polish pass over the whole UI and a fix for RestedXP 4.11.39. New releases land about once a day.

## v0.3.4 — the crest for every class

Estimated: within a few days.

Every class tracks what it needs around the crest, so there is one place to look.

Highlights:

- Your crest tracks your buffs, procs, cooldowns and your effects on the target, for every class and spec.
- Tanks get threat and their mitigation, healers get their heals, shields and mana.
- Every form, stance and spec gets its own full crest, each piece with its own switch.
