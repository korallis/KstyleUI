# KstyleUI roadmap

What's coming next, release by release. Dates are estimates; we'll keep this post up to date as each release ships. v0.2.14 is out now: flask and low-mana reminders, more party and raid frame options, unit frame aura settings that stick in every layout, and another round of KstyleUI doing less work so it runs smoother. New releases now land about once a day.

## v0.2.15 — the last frame options

Estimated: within a day or two.

The last party and raid frame options, and fixes from your reports.

Highlights:

- Party and raid frames: anchor and show-on-bottom choices, indicator match mode, and frame sorting where Forever allows.
- Movable Blizzard windows and a Time Spiral alert, once they are checked in game.
- More fixes from your reports, released as soon as they are ready.

## v0.3.0 — the polish release

Estimated: about a week after v0.2.15.

A full polish pass over everything you see and touch, so KstyleUI feels premium from the first minute.

Highlights:

- One typeface voice across the Medallions HUD, cards, chat and tooltips, and one lit glass finish on every bar.
- Readable everywhere: small text over the world sits on a soft backing, and names and timers stay clear in busy fights.
- A clean screen at rest: panels fade out of combat and come straight back on hover, target or combat.
- Casts by their caster and interrupt states you can read at a glance.
- Medallions finish: slab end caps and a calmer resting crest.
- A smoother Layout Mode and Options: search that takes you there, live previews and one motion style.
- A better first run: big HUD pictures, one-click Looks, and Medallions as the default for new profiles.
- A notice if anything fails to load, plus an About health page.

## v0.3.1 — the explorer release

Estimated: about a week after v0.3.0.

The world map becomes a full travel companion.

Highlights:

- Find the nearest innkeeper, trainer, bank, auction house, repair or reagent vendor, and route there.
- Click a quest on the quest card to route straight to it.
- Optionally reveal unexplored areas of the map.
- More travel stops: every boat, zeppelin, portal and your own hearth and class teleports on the route.

## Later

- Item levels on equipment slots and a gamepad-friendly layout.
- More languages polished by native speakers.
