# Medallions effects layer

The effects layer is the part of the Medallions HUD style (KSTY-87 section 6, KSTY-88, M7) that every Medallions effect runs through. It decides how much may move, which effect owns a place on screen, and when a 3D model may play. Every effect is an AnimationGroup, a flipbook or an engine curve: nothing visual runs Lua per frame. Nothing here runs under Frames.

| File | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `Core/Effects.lua` | The motion level, `Allows`, the arbiter, the `Medallions` settings section |
| `Core/EffectModels.lua` | The 3D model pool, `Model`, `Burst`, the candidate file IDs |
| `Core/EffectBursts.lua` | The player medallion's level-up burst and the target bracket glows' motion |
| `Core/Vignette.lua` | The screen-edge low-health vignette |
| `Core/Layout/Rest.lua` | The leave-combat rest of the secondary frames |
| `KstyleUI_ActionBars/SlabEffects.lua` | The slot ready flash, the hub's resource-cap ring, the hub's 3D proc and level-up models |
| `KstyleUI_ActionBars/SlabGlyphs.lua` | The rune band's glyph tide and stance mantle ([below](#glyph-tide-and-stance-mantle)) |
| `KstyleUI_UnitFrames/CastEffects.lua` | The cast bar's landed and stopped flash |
| `Core/Widgets/Medallion.lua` (M5) | The sheen as ambient motion, the flip-in's claim, `Medallion:Burst` |
| `KstyleUI_ActionBars/Slab.lua` (M6) | The hub's ambient sheen and particles yield while an effect holds the hub |

## Motion level

General > Medallions > Medallions motion is Full (the default), Calm or Off, saved per profile as `Medallions.motion` through `Store.RegisterDefaults`. The level in force is the lower of that setting and Appearance > Motion: Reduced caps it at Calm and Off makes it Off, so the existing reduced-motion setting still wins.

| Kind | Full | Calm | Off | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ambient` | yes | yes | no | The hub's rune turn, breathing glow and sheen; the portrait sheen |
| `flare` | yes | yes | no | One-shot 2D effects: flip-in, combat sweep, proc start, hub pulse, level-up burst, ready flash, cast flash, bracket glow fade-in |
| `pulse` | yes | no | no | Repeating event loops: the slot's proc flipbook, the low-health ring, the vignette's and cap ring's breathing, the bracket glow's breathing |
| `particle` | yes | no | no | The hub's class particles |
| `model` | yes | no | no | 3D models |

At Off an effect's end state is applied at once (the secondary frames dim), and a state such as the low-health ring, the vignette or the cap ring still shows, still.

Two switches on the same page, both on by default, let a player drop an effect's drawing cost without touching the rest (`Medallions.vignette`, `Medallions.ambient`):

- **Low-health vignette** off hides the vignette's 16 edge slices, so they leave the draw (health is secret, so Lua cannot hide them by health). The portrait's low-health ring still shows and keeps breathing: it breathes on the vignette's group, so that one group runs on while the ring uses it and stops otherwise.
- **Ambient hub effects** off stills the hub as Calm does: the rune turn, glow and bloom breaths, sheen and particles stop, and the sheen and particles are hidden. The static hub art stays, and flares (combat, proc, level-up, ready and cast flashes) still play by the motion level.

**Calm crest at rest** (`Medallions.restingCrest`, on by default) settles the hub while the player is out of combat with no target, no proc and no effect holding it: the rune ring pauses where it stands, the sheen finishes its pass, the particles go, and only the glow and bloom breathe, at half speed (8.4 s a breath). Combat, a target or a proc wakes it to full at once, the runes turning on from their angle, and a wake during the sheen's last pass restarts the sheen. A target or proc repaints the hub only when ambient motion is on and its rest actually changes. It only stops loops, so the idle hub runs 2 loops instead of 9 (`test-perf-medallions-switches`). Off keeps the full idle motion.

| Function | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `Effects.Level() -> "full" \| "calm" \| "off"` | The level in force |
| `Effects.Allows(kind) -> boolean` | Whether that kind of motion may play now |
| `Effects.Active() -> boolean` | Whether the HUD style is Medallions |
| `EffectsLevelChanged(level, old)` | A callback, fired once when the level in force changes from either setting |

## Priority and arbitration

A place is any key: `"hub"`, a slot button, a medallion, a cast bar. One effect holds a place at a time. The priorities are KSTY-87's table (`Effects.PRIORITY`, lower wins): low health 1; proc, level-up and medallion burst 2; ready flash and cast flash 3; resource cap and target change 4; entering combat 5; leaving combat 6; ambient 9.

| Function | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `Claim(place, priority, stop) -> token \| nil` | Takes the place when it is free or held at an equal or lower priority (the holder's `stop` runs); refuses a lower priority |
| `ClaimFor(place, priority, seconds, stop)` | A claim the layer releases after `seconds` (`C_Timer.After`) |
| `Release(place, token)`, `Stop(place)` | Frees the place; a replaced claim's token frees nothing |
| `Holder(place) -> priority \| nil` | Who holds the place |
| `Ambient(place, start, stop)` | Ambient motion for a place: it runs while the place is free and `Allows("ambient")`, and `stop` pauses it while any effect holds the place |

Who claims what: a proc holds its slot (a ready flash on it yields, and the proc ends a running flash); a ready flash holds its slot for 0.3 s; a cast flash holds its cast bar; a flip-in holds its medallion for 0.18 s and a burst for 1.2 s, pausing the portrait sheen; the target lock's claws hold the hub for 0.8 s at the target-change priority; a proc model and the level-up model hold the hub, pausing the hub's sheen and particles (the rune ring and breathing keep turning, so nothing snaps). Every claim is allocation-free on its combat path (`Tools/regression/perf/`).

## 3D models

`Effects.Model(anchor, key, size, seconds, priority, fallback, place)` plays a model from a pool of at most three ModelScenes. A scene is `MARGIN` (1.6) times the effect's size, so the model never clips, takes its anchor's strata and level + 2 (the hub's `LOW`, so slots stay above it), fades in over 0.1 s and out over 0.3 s, and is then paused (`SetPaused`), cleared and hidden. A full pool evicts its oldest model of the lowest priority when the newcomer's priority is equal or higher, and refuses otherwise. The pool is built on first use, so a reload builds it again. A model plays only at Full; otherwise, when the pool refuses, when `SetModelByFileID` returns false, or when the actor has not loaded 0.5 s later, the caller's 2D twin plays instead. A file that is refused, or misses its load twice, is not tried again this session.

`Effects.Burst(place, priority, anchor, key, size, seconds, play2D, stop2D)` claims the place for `seconds` and plays the model or the 2D twin; it returns `"model"`, `"2d"` or false.

The candidates are referenced by file ID and ship nothing. Each ID was checked against the community listfile (wowdev/wow-listfile, 2026-11-15); the 70862 UI source holds no models, so whether Forever's data has each one is an in-game check.

## Glyph tide and stance mantle

`KstyleUI_ActionBars/SlabGlyphs.lua` (`ns.SlabGlyphs`) owns the rune band's two state effects. `SlabEffects.UpdateCap` calls `Glyphs.Update()` whenever the crest shows, hides or restyles (and on the effects level), and its `Disable` calls it once more, so neither keeps an event when the crest is gone. Their parts are built once, out of combat only (`SlabMedals.Locked()`); work the lock or a secret held back (a build, a secret power type, a secret form) runs again on `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` or `ADDON_RESTRICTION_STATE_CHANGED`. Neither runs OnUpdate or touches a region under an engine aura button, and every part lands on whole pixels (`Widgets.Fill`, `Widgets.Point`). With both switches off the crest is exactly as before: the band unturned, unmasked, at its own alpha and in the class colour, and the cap ring the old one.

**Glyph tide** (ActionBars > Slab and cooldowns > Glyph tide, `crestTide`, on by default; Medallions only). Two vertical `StatusBar`s over the band, inset 6 units top and bottom so their range covers the glyphs, take `UnitPower("player", kind)` over `0..UnitPowerMax` for the main power (`UnitPowerType`), raw or secret: one with `ExponentialEaseOut` on a power event (`Immediate` at Off and when built), the other always `Immediate`. Their fills draw nothing; a mask anchored to each fill (`CLAMPTOBLACKADDITIVE`, the pattern `Blizzard_UIWidgetTemplateFillUpFrames.lua:206` uses for its vertical fill-up bars) crops the lit layers, and a layer under both masks shows only below the lower fill, so a gain eases and a spend drops at once with no Lua compare. The lit layers are the full `medRuneGlyphs` ring in the power bar's colour (`Theme.Palette` or `PowerBarColor`, through `Theme.Brighten`, as the unit frames paint it), a paler core of the same strokes (the colour 55% toward white, at 0.6) and a fringe over the band (`ring`, 146 units, at 0.25), all added. A third mask, from the immediate fill's top to the hub's top, keeps the band's turning glyphs above the tide only, so a lit glyph never doubles; it comes off whenever the tide hides. A mana user's tide shows in combat only, reads and writes nothing while hidden (it is fed afresh when combat starts), and settles away over 0.6 s when combat ends (at once at Off). While it shows, the cap ring tracks its power and is its last step (the Resource cap row).

**Stance mantle** (`crestMantle`, on by default; warrior, druid, rogue, priest, shaman and paladin, the classes with a form or stance on Forever). `GetShapeshiftFormID()` picks a tint:

| Form | ID | Tint (token) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battle, Defensive, Berserker Stance | 17, 18, 19 | `gilt`, `magic` (steel blue), `schoolFire` (ember) |
| Bear and Dire Bear, Cat, Travel Form | 5 and 8, 1, 3 | `pipDruidGlow` (amber), `coinGold`, `mantleTravel` (teal) |
| Stealth | 30 | `schoolShadow`, and the emblem dims to 0.55 |
| Shadowform | 28 | `schoolShadow` |
| Ghost Wolf | 16 | `totemSpirit` (pale blue) |
| Any other form | | The class colour |

No form (0) leaves today's band. A worn mantle tints the band's turning glyphs (`hub.runes`) and lifts them from 0.25 to 0.7, and lights a rim on the band's inner edge (`ringLine32` 122 units across, at full alpha). Every glyph layer turns together: the tide's lit layers and the old ink join the band's own turn (Rotation animations on its group, `SetTarget`), so lit and unlit stay in phase. A change of form ID (Bear to Dire Bear too) turns the whole band one notch, 30 degrees in 0.3 s easing out, and keeps the angle (`SetRotation` on every layer when the turn ends; an interrupted turn lands first); cross-fades the old ink (a copy of the band in the old tint) out as the new fades in over 0.25 s easing in and out; lifts the old rim off as a ripple 10 units outward that fades in 0.35 s easing out (in the new tint when there was none); and fades the new rim and the emblem's dimming in over 0.25 s. The first paint after a reload moves nothing; Motion Off stops every effect in flight and shows each end state, the notch included. Switching the mantle off turns the band back to no notch. Under Frames the active stance button's rim takes the tint (`Button.SetAccent` honours `b.mantle`), and every other button keeps the accent. With both on, the band shows the stance's tint above the tide line and the power's colour below it.

Tested by `test-actionbars-medallions-glyphs`; rendered with `RENDER_FX=tide` (`RENDER_TIDE=from,to` in percent) and `RENDER_FX=mantle` (`RENDER_MANTLE=from,to` form IDs, under either HUD style) ([render README](../Tools/render/README.md)).

## Crest medals and sockets

`KstyleUI_ActionBars/SlabMedals.lua` (`ns.SlabMedals`, loaded before the hawks, storm orbs and totems) holds the crest pieces the class trackers share. Positions and sizes are in hub units (the 150-unit ring); `Place` snaps them and makes every part an even number of pixels. A piece's `lits` are its engine-driven layers; Lua never reads or compares what they show.

| Call | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Known(spells)` | The first spell in the list the player knows, or nil |
| `Restricted()`, `Locked()` | `Restricted()` is true while auras are secret: call `Engine` only when it is false. `Locked()` adds combat lockdown: build and size pieces only when it is false (wait for `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`), so nothing under an engine aura button is resized in combat even while auras are readable; prepared choices still swap in combat |
| `Round(frame)`, `Texture(parent, layer, sublevel, token, color, units, blend)`, `Layer(holder, level)`, `Even(region)`, `Steps(target, steps, looping)`, `Cloud(parent, token, cell)`, `Flip(texture, token, stretch)` | The parts the hawk perches and storm orbs were built from |
| `Mark(piece, parent, art, size, color, r, g, b, level, lights)` | One crest mark: a flat texture is drawn additive in `color` (or `r, g, b`), as before. A texture flagged `forged` in `Core/Tokens.lua` (the fourth value of `Tokens.Texture`; a Blender render in neutral metal) is drawn solid under a thin additive sheen: at 24 units and over, gold (`coinGold`) on a soft shadow with a halo 1.7 times its size in the spell's colour behind it; under 24 units, solid in the colour the caller passes, where a tint reads better than a halo: the spell's colour for a Sting in the Frames row, white for a forged cooldown sigil so it reads on its medal's dark face. `level` puts a forged mark on a layer of its own so one bloom scales every part. A forged texture with an `aura` (the fifth value of `Tokens.Texture`: a Blender flip-book token with `rows`, `columns`, `frames` and `seconds`, as the class ambient sheets have, and an optional `span`, the fifth value of `Tokens.Flipbook`, baked by `Tools/media/forge/auras.py`) draws that aura instead of the sheen (`Medals.Aura`), in white because its colour is baked, or in `color` (or `r, g, b`) when its flip-book token sets `tint` (the sixth value of `Tokens.Flipbook`), as a sheet every class shares or a piece whose colour changes in play is baked white: a fire that loops the sheet as an engine FlipBook and a still on its first cell. A seated aura burns over the empty socket (`auraSocket`, gold at 24 units and over, else in the colour); one with a `span` above 1 is bare and burns over its own forged figure drawn black. The fire shows only while `ambient` is allowed and the crest is awake; Calm, Off and the resting crest show the still. ## Readability floor

Every state-carrying part of a crest piece is 16 hub units or more, about 29 pixels at 1080 high: a socket's gem (a 22-unit gilt setting, an 18-unit lip and a 16-unit gem, the threat gem's too), a pip (`Medals.PIP` = 16, set round its medal 2 units apart, or along the band at 63 units out), a vial, a drop or a brand, a cooldown sigil (16 across, three to a line, 20 apart), a foot medal (`Medals.FOOT` = 16), a druid's Clearcasting leaf and a shaman's imbue stone. Glows, settings and shades may be smaller; the part that fills, drains or counts may not. `test-actionbars-crest-floor.lua` boots every class in every form with every spell known and measures every fill bar, shown cooldown sweep, sigil, leaf and stone.

## Procs

A proc (mage Clearcasting, warlock Shadow Trance, druid Clearcasting) is a rim piece with `proc = <colour token>`: when it lights, `Medals.Rim`'s flare is a thin ring (`ringLine16`, 150 across) in the proc's colour, under the same motion gate as every flare, and while it is up the rim holds the proc aura (`procAura`, `Media/Textures/Medallions/Auras/ProcAura.png`), a Blender-rendered flipbook of three bright arcs running round a ring of light with orbiting sparks, tinted in the proc's colour: it loops at Full and holds its first frame at Calm and at rest, like any rim's flipbook. There is no class burst and no ambient fire. `Tools/media/proc_aura.py` renders and bakes the aura. The Medallions cooldown rails light the same aura in gilt round a proc's medal (`Widgets.MedallionsProc`), built the first time a medal procs, looping only while Medallions motion allows ambient effects. The burst and the fire belong to the major cooldowns' blaze ([Unleashed](#unleashed)), so a proc never reads as one. The rune glyphs and halo a mage's or warlock's moment draws stay in the proc's colour.

## Seats

`SlabSeats.lua` is the crest's seat registry: every point a piece sits on has one name, and a seat holds one owner at a time. `Seats.Name(seat)` turns a tracker's `seat` into a name: a `Medals.Seat` name stays itself, and an `{ x, y }` seat takes the named seat within 12 hub units of it, so the warrior's groove and the warlock's socket on the lower left are the same seat. `Seats.Claim(order, specs, crest)` walks the shown pieces in row order before the trackers lay them out: the first piece on a seat holds it, and a later one is left off the crest with a developer log line naming the seat and its holder. Ring layers (`"rim"`, `"cooldowns"`, `"plaque"`), pieces with their own `place` and pieces marked `overlay` claim nothing: an overlay draws over its seat's owner, such as the hunter's Mark reticle and the low-level strike flashes over the emblem the Shot holds. The Frames row claims no seats. `test-actionbars-crest-seats.lua` boots every class in every form with every spell known and checks that no piece is left off and every point seat has a name.

## Crest trackers

## Crest rail

`KstyleUI_ActionBars/SlabRail.lua` stands a class's major abilities as forged medals above the crest (Action Bars > Slab and cooldowns > Cooldown rail, on by default, Medallions only). The hunter, the mage and the warlock have a rail; each class registers its abilities and trees with `SlabRail.Register` in `SlabRanged.lua`. The main rail holds five medals: four abilities and the on-use trinket in slot 13, else 14, in the last seat (Trinkets on the crest still switches it). The talent tree the game names picks the seats (`C_SpecializationInfo.GetSpecialization`, `Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/SpecializationInfoDocumentation.lua`); where that answers nothing, the tree with the most points spent (`GetSpecializationInfo`'s seventh return), and where that is missing too, the tree whose signature spells the character knows most of. Only known spells get a seat. The tree's long cooldowns sit first; a tree with fewer than four fills its free seats with its rotation cooldowns, then with the class's other long cooldowns. A long cooldown beyond the four seats, and a known racial, stands as a smaller round medal on a second rail beside the crest (at most six). Spending talent points or switching talent groups (`PLAYER_TALENT_UPDATE`, `ACTIVE_TALENT_GROUP_CHANGED`) seats the new tree; a seat dropped that way stops its aura and anything in flight. The seats are chosen afresh only while the crest can be rebuilt (out of combat and with auras readable), so the other trackers never give up an ability or the trinket in combat. A new choice is offered to the rail before the other trackers build, and once the update ends they keep only what a shown rail placed: a rail the update could not build or seat hands their cues back in a second pass. Switching the rail off while the crest cannot be rebuilt keeps the rail showing until the cooldown rim's sigils, Rapid Fire's gust and the crest trinket can take its cues back, while every other tracker updates as usual; switching it on in combat leaves those cues where they are until the rail can be placed.

The lists follow the crest plan's per-tree ledger, built from Forever class guides (Icy Veins, Wowhead and Warcraft Tavern for the Forever client) and the client's own Cooldown Manager sets, with each spell's ranks checked against Forever's spell data. A tree's confirmed long cooldowns sit first, in the ledger's order, and its rotation cooldowns fill only the seats left free. An ability that already has its own crest piece takes no seat: Bane of Doom stays on its torment spire, Arcane Wrath on the mage's school arc and Conflagrate in its socket. ## Mage crest rail

The orb's own cooldown is never seated. No mage seat rises to the crown: the mage's kit names no buff for a seat to crown, so a pressed seat dips, bursts and cools in place.

| Tree (signature) | Seats, in order, known ones only |
| --- | --- |
| Arcane (Arcane Power, Presence of Mind) | Presence of Mind, Arcane Power, Evocation, Blink, Frost Nova, Fire Blast, Ice Block, Cold Snap, Ice Barrier, Combustion, Blast Wave, Ice Lance |
| Fire (Combustion, Blast Wave) | Combustion, Blast Wave, Fire Blast, Blink, Frost Nova, Evocation, Ice Block, Cold Snap, Ice Barrier, Arcane Power, Presence of Mind, Ice Lance |
| Frost (Cold Snap, Ice Barrier, Ice Block) | Cold Snap, Ice Block, Ice Barrier, Ice Lance, Frost Nova, Blink, Evocation, Fire Blast, Arcane Power, Presence of Mind, Combustion, Blast Wave |

The racials follow: Perception, Eureka!, Escape Artist, Blood Fury, Shatter, Will of the Forsaken, Berserking and the troll's Rapid Regeneration, whichever the player knows. Touch of the Grave is passive, so it takes no seat. While the rail shows, Frost Nova's and Fire Blast's cooldowns stand on it, not on the left band or the lower left. A rail of more than five seats flattens its arc: seats 31 units apart, 133 units above the hub's centre in the middle and 119 at the ninth seat's ends, clear of the unit frames and under the cooldown row (`test-actionbars-crest-ranged-reach`).

## Hawk perch motion

The hunter's hawk perches (`KstyleUI_ActionBars/SlabHawks.lua`, [ActionBars README](../KstyleUI_ActionBars/README.md#hawk-perches)) are the reference for a tracker that pops off the crest. Each perch keeps two anchors: the seat on the ring's shoulder, which holds the steel hawk, the timer ring, its ember and the ripple, and a lift layer that holds the lit hawk (its figure drawn black under its animated aura) and its flare. The aura loops only while its perch is lit, at Full with the crest awake; Calm, Off and the resting crest show its still, and an empty or expired perch plays nothing. Only the lift layer travels, so the timer never moves while the player reads it.

| Moment | Full | Calm | Off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summon (a hawk in a free slot, or a third summon refreshing the oldest) | The burning hawk fades in over 0.06 s, rises 13 units outward along the perch's shoulder (40 degrees from straight up, mirrored on the right) and swells from 0.55 to 1.35 times in 0.12 s (easing out), then settles home to its own size over 0.30 s (the shrink easing in, as every step that leaves does); a hunter-green ring ripples from the seat to 1.5 times and fades in 0.35 s; the flare plays | The flare only: no travel, swell or ripple | The burning hawk and timer show at once |
| Hawk out | The steel hawk hides under the burning one, so it never shows through | Same | Same |
| Two hawks out | The rune glyphs and the halo fade in over 0.30 s, then breathe | They fade in over 0.30 s, with no breath | They switch on; switching to Off mid-fade shows them lit at once |
| Third summon | The refreshed perch lifts its new hawk while the old one sinks; the glow stays lit, with no second fade | The flare only | Instant |
| Expiry | The gilt hawk sinks 3 units towards the crest while it fades over 0.35 s, and the steel hawk returns | The fade only | The steel hawk returns at once |

The lift, ripple and sink play only at Full, and only while no proc or level-up holds the hub (`Effects.Holder("hub")` at `PRIORITY.proc` or lower), so the urgent effect keeps the motion, and a proc or level-up that arrives mid-lift stops the travel, swell, ripple and sink where they are (`Hawks.Yield`); the hawk still lights, flares and drains. Summon Hawk's own cast flash (`PRIORITY.cast`) does not hold it. Appearance > Motion set to reduced caps the perches at Calm. Every move is an engine animation group started from `PLAYER_TOTEM_UPDATE`; no perch runs Lua per frame.

In combat the totem slot's boolean is secret. The steel hawk then hides through `SetAlphaFromBoolean`, the lift plays under a ripple gate the engine draws from the same boolean, and the pair's glow switches without its fade, because Lua cannot tell a refresh from a new pair. The lifted hawk reaches at most 13 units past its seat, which stays clear of the action slots, the level badge, the unit frames and the cast bars at 1280x720, 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3440x1440, 5120x1440 and 3840x2160 (`test-actionbars-crest-ranged-reach`, `test-actionbars-crest-ranged-reach-1440`, `test-actionbars-crest-ranged-reach-wide`).

[Crest coverage](crest-coverage.md) lists which cooldowns and procs reach the crest, why the others are left out, and which of them the Cooldown Manager row is only a candidate fallback for.

## Crest motion rules

Every crest effect follows the same rules, and `test-actionbars-crest-motion.lua` holds them.

- **One end-warning flicker.** `Tokens.Flicker` (alpha 1 to 0.6 and back in two 0.25 s steps, eased both ways) is the only fast blink on the crest; `ns.Slab.Flicker(target, alpha)` builds it. A dimmer part flickers between its own alpha and 0.6 of it.
- **Eased fades and steps.** `ns.Slab.Fade` and `ns.Slab.Grow` take their curve from `Tokens.Smoothing` through `ns.Slab.Ease`: ease out when a part appears or grows, ease in when it leaves, both ways in a loop. Travel along a path (orbits, sweeps, particles) keeps a constant speed.
- **Warning colours come late.** Amber shows only in a timer's last fifth and red in its last twelfth (see Expiring ember below).
- **Calm keeps the cues as still marks.** At Calm a ready flare shows without moving: its art stands at full alpha for one 0.45 s pass, whether Lua or an aura slot starts it. The refresh and end warning holds the part that flickers at Full at the flicker's low alpha (0.6 of its own) for as long as the warning lasts: `ns.Slab.Still(flicker)` is the engine's pandemic animation on every slot that has a flicker (figures, the ranged kit's mark, stacks, band and spire), and `ns.Slab.Warn(flicker, on)` does the same for a warning Lua plays (the vigil's last charge, the last storm orb, a totem spire's last fifth, a shaman's rune stone in its last five minutes, and the readable last fifth of a horn, a rim or the cooldown ring). A ranged piece that blooms as it lands holds its sigil at the bloom's starting size for one pass instead. Loops and travel stop. Off shows end states only.
- **Endings fold in combat.** Under hidden values an ember's gilt fade starts from the game timer's own `OnCooldownDone`, and the Unleashed corona folds the frame after the last lit hot line's game timer runs out or the game clears its slot's aura (see Unleashed below).
- **One flare at a time.** Every crest flare claims the `crestFlare` place in the effects arbiter at `ready` priority as it starts, so a newer flare stops the older one: a cooldown coming ready, an aura slot's flare or Unleashed burst (claimed from the group's `OnPlay`, which runs when the engine plays it too), and the hawk, storm and totem flourishes, and a warlock's spent wisps, each of which claims once for the whole ensemble so its members play together. While aura values are hidden Lua may not touch anything inside an aura slot, so there a flare the engine is playing is hidden by its own layer when a newer flare starts (a medal's flare and the Unleashed burst each sit in a slot and under a layer of their own), and the newer one shows at once. A ranged piece's bloom is the growth of the sigil's own art, so there the piece shows a copy of its slots that has no cue until the bloom's 0.5 s are over: the sigil stays and only its growth is hidden.
- **A level changed in combat shows at once.** A slot keeps the cues it was bound with until the restriction ends, so every level has its slot bound beforehand. An aura medal's flare has two slots under two layers the addon owns, one with the flare and one with the still mark: Full lights the first, Calm the second, Off neither. A piece with a cue on its own art (a refresh flicker, a bloom, a loop on a hunter's, mage's, warlock's, priest's, shaman's or druid's piece) has three sets of its slots, one bound for each level, each under a layer of its own, and the level in use lights its set. The copies are slots only, anchored on the piece, and cost two more aura feeds for each such piece; a slot a piece adds beside its first one (a mage's stack embers, the hunter's gust, a warlock's torment spires) sits under the same layer as the first, so it changes with it. A loop outside these pieces has a moving copy and a still copy instead, each in a slot under a layer of its own, with the loop bound once to the moving copy: the storm halo's clouds and each orb's spark (`SlabStorm.lua`), a druid's Clearcasting leaves (`SlabGrove.lua`) the flash of a healer's cooldown sigil (a glow in a slot of its own that only Full lights) and a cooldown sigil's aura (its fire in a slot that Full lights while the crest is awake, its still in one lit otherwise). A cue that began while its layer was dark took no flare place, so a level change, or the Unleashed switch, does not bring it on screen half played: its layer stays dark for the rest of the cue's time (`Medals.Reveal`). The two one-shot cues with a moving copy follow the same rule: a sigil flash or a leaf unfurl that began unseen stays dark until it has ended, with the sigil and the still leaves on screen meanwhile. The sigils of one unit share the flash's layer, so a flash that begins while the layer is dark keeps it dark for its own whole time. Both copies of the leaves stand on the same ring. `test-actionbars-crest-motion.lua` walks every class at Calm and Off under hidden values and fails on any slot that can show while it still holds a moving cue.
- **Quiet loops.** The warrior's horn calls once when Battle Shout lands and once as its last fifth begins (while Lua can read the aura; one call for each application, with Expiring ember on or off, and at once for a shout first seen inside that fifth), and a shaman's storm orbs stand still in their seats while the crest rests.

Under Frames each point or stack lights its share (60% over the row) of the pip row's glow, in `KstyleUI_UnitFrames/Pips.lua`: Lua-fed bars for the combo row, application bars on the fed crown's container otherwise (not the paladin's Vengeance row). The switch, a profile change and a palette change repaint the steps only, with no relayout; bars under an aura button wait for auras to be readable and combat to end (`PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED`, `ADDON_RESTRICTION_STATE_CHANGED`).

## In-game checks

The render harness cannot draw models or run the client, so sixteen things wait for Lee's single test. Each says what to look for and what happens when it fails; none of them can break the HUD, because every one already has its fallback in code.

1. **PNG filtering at 2:1.** Look at the hub's rune ring and a slot's ready flash at 1920x1080 and 5120x1440. Works: the 1 px edges stay crisp and the glow has no stair steps. Fails (soft or shimmering edges): the textures ship at the size they draw, and M3 redraws the affected ones procedurally (KSTY-87 5.3).
2. **Model file IDs and their frame cost.** Turn Medallions motion to Full, level up or use a spell with a proc (`/run KstyleUI.Effects.Burst("hub", 2, KstyleUI.Effects.playerMedallion, "levelUp", 150, 1.2)` also works). Works: golden level-up rays at the hub and the class model behind the medallion, with `/run print(KstyleUI.Effects.LiveModels())` never above 3 and the frame rate steady. Fails (nothing shows, or a model is huge, tiny or off-centre): the 2D burst plays instead after 0.5 s; tell Capy which class, and the camera distance or the ID changes in one table.
3. **OnCooldownDone in combat.** Use a spell with a cooldown longer than the global cooldown in combat. Works: one steel flash on its slot when it comes back, and none after a plain global cooldown. Fails (no flash in combat): the slot's `readyEffect` stays, and the fix is the cooldown frame's own `SetDrawBling` with `medReadyFlash` as its bling texture.
4. **Curve alpha.** Drop below 40% health, and fill your rage or energy. Works: the red edge grows as health falls (full at 12%) and the hub's ring lights while the resource is full, both with no error. Fails (always on, always off, or a Lua error about a secret): the vignette and ring hide and the effect stays a 2D ring pulse only; tell Capy which.
5. **The Skyborne race file.** Make or log in a Skyborne character of each faction and run `/run print(UnitRace("player"))`. Works: one race file for both (the one-heraldry ruling). Fails (two files): M2 adds the second file's art and `Core/Medallions.lua` maps both to it.
6. **The kill window's crossing.** As a warrior with Execute (or a paladin with Hammer of Wrath), hit a target from 30% down past 20%. Works: the foot of the rim fades in red with one wave dropping outward as the target crosses under 20%, then beats, and later hits inside the window send no second wave. Fails (no wave, or a wave on every hit): the client fires `OnValueChanged` differently for a secret value; the burn itself still follows the health, and the fix is to drop the status bar and keep the band's curve alpha only.
7. **Ring-edge timers.** Cast Shadow Bolt and be interrupted once; swing in melee; turn Global cooldown spark on; let a buff run down out of combat and in combat. Works: the cast arc fills with a bright spark at its head on the rim and passes under the level badge, the swing ring's head is a spark on the rim, the spark rides the edge with no line through the crest, and in both a timer shows no warning colour until its last fifth, amber alone from there and red alone in its last twelfth. The heads are `RingBead.png` as each Cooldown's circular edge (`SetEdgeTexture`); the harness draws it as a bead on the ring with its trail along the tangent, which the client's own placement must confirm. Fails (a line from the centre, a stretched bead, or no ember in combat): tell Capy which; `SetDrawEdge(false)` removes the heads in one line and the arcs still drain.
8. **The glyph tide's mask.** Fill rage or energy part way. Works: the band's glyphs light from the foot of the ring to a level line that rises with the power, crisp and unsquashed. Fails (the whole ring lights, or none does): the mask did not follow the fill; tell Capy, and Glyph tide off restores today's band.
9. **The stance mantle's notch.** Change stance or form. Works: the band turns one notch smoothly and stays there while it keeps turning slowly, and the halo changes colour. Fails (the band jumps back 30 degrees after the turn, or turns twice): the client does not keep `SetRotation` under a playing Rotation; tell Capy, and Stance mantle off restores today's band.
10. **A paladin's auras as forms.** As a paladin, change aura. Works either way: if Forever lists auras as forms the band turns and a paladin-pink halo lights; if not, nothing changes. Tell Capy which, so the aura colours can be chosen.
11. **Unleashed's engine loops.** Pop a major cooldown (Recklessness, Adrenaline Rush, Arcane Power) in combat at Full. Works: the class burst grows out of the ring, the corona burns and breathes on its edge over a warm glow, the glyphs blaze and turn, the thin line drains, and as the buff ends the corona folds inward and the glyphs cool instead of cutting; with two cooldowns up (or a hunter's and the pet's) the fold waits for the last, also when the second starts in the moment the first runs out. At a boss pull, where auras turn secret before combat starts, pop one as the pull begins: the burst, corona and glyphs light as above, nothing folds until the last cooldown ends in the fight, the corona folds then, and no Lua error shows. In combat, switch Unleashed off, pop a major cooldown, switch it back on and let the cooldown run out: the corona folds. Then pop one, switch Unleashed off until it has ended and switch it back on: nothing folds. As a druid, pop Barkskin in combat in caster form, Cat Form and Bear Form in turn, and once shift form while it runs: the corona folds as it ends in whichever form shows, and nothing folds at the shift. Fails (the corona or glyphs stand still, the fold plays while another cooldown is still up, no fold for the cooldown popped while switched off, a fold as the switch comes back after the cooldown ended, a druid's crest that folds only in Bear Form, or a Lua error about an aura button): the still slot shows the same corona and glyphs without motion, so set the motion to Calm and tell Capy.

Fails (the ring squashes instead of cropping while it sweeps): the end state is right either way, because every step is a whole bar; binding the steps `Immediate` drops the sweep.
13. **The hawk lift.** As a hunter at Full, summon a hawk, a second, a third, and let one expire, once out of combat and once in combat. Works: each new hawk, burning in its animated aura, rises off its shoulder and settles while its timer ring stays still, the empty steel hawk never shows under a gilt one, the glow fades in for two hawks and stays lit through the third summon, and an expired hawk sinks back to steel. Fails (no lift in combat, or a steel hawk showing under a gilt one): the slot's secret boolean did not reach the gate; tell Capy, and Calm keeps the flare and timers with no lift.
14. **The crest's motion rules.** At Full, let a buff with a crest timer run down out of combat and in combat, let two cooldowns come ready within half a second, and as a warrior cast Battle Shout and wait for its last fifth; then repeat at Calm. Works: every end warning blinks at the same two beats a second; no ring shows amber before its last fifth, and red replaces amber (never over it) in the last twelfth; only one ready flare shows at a time; the horn calls once on the cast and once near the end; at Calm a ready cue stands still for a moment and a warning dims its part without blinking.
